# 2026.323.0252 — fix(cuda)!: drop the layer-nvidia require from the CUDA toolkit

## Summary

`opencharly/layer-cuda` is the CUDA **toolkit** — runtime, compute libraries, `nvcc`.
`opencharly/layer-nvidia` is the **driver** — the user-space libraries, the container toolkit and
the `nvidia-ctk` that configures them. The `require:` between them was a category error: it says
"every image that composes the toolkit also wants the driver", and it is target-blind, because
only the BOX knows which substrate it is.

**Why the dependency was the defect, and not the assertion.** opencharly/layer-nvidia#25
diagnosed both halves: `layer-nvidia`'s baked `check:` steps assert `/usr/bin/nvidia-ctk` exists,
and this layer's own `cuda-drop-driver-userspace` step deletes that binary (and every driver
library) from any image that composes both. So a pod could never pass the plan it inherited.
opencharly/spec#395 investigated the two mechanisms that could have made a baked check declare
itself inapplicable — a derived strip marker, and a per-step `when:` matcher over derived image
facts — and both were rejected; adding `pod`/`vm` words to `#Context` was rejected explicitly,
because "when" is not "where" and would not have fixed the `nvidia` base either. What remains is
the dependency itself: the toolkit layer must not carry the driver, because the substrate decides
who supplies it.

## Change classification

**Breaking change + rollback:** every image that composes `layer-cuda` without naming
`layer-nvidia` loses the driver layer, and every image that wants a baked driver must now say so
itself. Concretely: `distro-fedora/box/immich-ml-cdi`, `distro-arch/box/cuda-arch-builder`, and the
pod candies `pod-comfyui`, `pod-jupyter-ml`, `pod-marimo`, `pod-unsloth-studio`,
`layer-python-ml`, `layer-strata` and `layer-cdi` lose it and need to do nothing — that is the pod
contract, and `layer-cdi`'s ordering guarantee is unaffected because it requires the toolkit, not
the driver. `opencharly/vm-nvidia` (`charly.yml:52-53`), `cachyos-gpu`
(`distro-cachyos/charly.yml:2089`) and `charly-cachyos-app` (`:2181`) already name the driver
themselves and need to do nothing. The two pod boxes that still name it —
`distro-fedora/box/nvidia-cdi/charly.yml:14` and `distro-cachyos/box/nvidia-cdi/charly.yml:13` —
must drop the entry when they take the new tag (`distro-fedora#127`, `distro-cachyos#208`). The new
tag is the one `tag-on-merge` mints at merge (the next after `v2026.323.0138`, the release this
supersedes). Rollback: pin consumers back to `layer-cuda:v2026.323.0138`, which still carries the
`require:`, or revert the squash commit on `main`; the graph, not a consumer, is what changes.
